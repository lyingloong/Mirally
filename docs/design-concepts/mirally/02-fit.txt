Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original desktop assistant app icon concept, single isolated brand symbol
Primary request: Design a distinctive premium brand symbol for Mirally, a personal AI partner from MIRA (Machine Intelligence Research and Applications) Lab. Mirally combines MIRA and ally. The one idea of this concept is complementary collaboration: two capable partners fitting together while retaining their own identities.
Subject: Two complementary, substantial rounded geometric pieces fit around a single carefully drawn narrow negative-space seam, creating one compact, balanced emblem. Each piece follows the other with precision and ease. Explore an original asymmetric near-square overall silhouette with a gently stepped or bent central seam and subtly open outer corners. The interface between the two shapes is the signature. Balanced visual weight, clean surfaces, no extra center object, no literal puzzle-piece knobs. Simple enough to sketch from memory.
Style/medium: Elegant contemporary technology identity. Vector-friendly minimal geometry and very shallow satin dimensionality, restrained surface shading with precise edges. It must work in flat blue as well. Avoid inflated plastic or heavy glass effects.
Scene/backdrop: Plain uniform near-white #F7F9FC background, opaque.
Composition/framing: Square composition. A single centered compact mark, approximately 62 percent of the canvas width and height. Ample even whitespace. Straight-on front view. No typography or presentation board.
Color palette: Sky blue #199FE6 and a closely related lighter azure for the second piece, small deep-blue accents #0878B2 for surface definition. Colors should feel like one coherent blue family with adequate contrast against white.
Constraints: No imposed letter A or M. No words, numbers, lettering, watermark, surrounding app tile, border, environment, or additional symbols. No yin-yang, heart, shield, handshake, human silhouettes, chain links, puzzle toy, infinity knot, folded wing, brain, or gear. Keep visible white separation and broad identifiable shapes at 24 pixels.
