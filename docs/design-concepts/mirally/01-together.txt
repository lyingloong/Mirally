Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original desktop assistant app icon concept, single isolated brand symbol
Primary request: Design a distinctive, refined symbol for Mirally, a personal AI partner from MIRA (Machine Intelligence Research and Applications) Lab. Mirally combines MIRA and ally. The one idea of this concept is two equals moving forward together: a person and their intelligent partner, sharing intention and momentum.
Subject: Two substantial, fluid, gently rising blue strokes traveling in the same direction with an even, deliberate white channel between them. Each stroke has its own clear beginning and ending. Together they form one compact, confident, memorable silhouette with a subtle upward-forward gesture. Equal visual weight and close rapport, no enclosing container. The overall shape should be an original abstract emblem, not a literal letter or recognizable existing logo. Make the partnership visible in the geometry, not in illustrative objects.
Style/medium: Sophisticated contemporary technology brand, vector-friendly geometry, precise rounded edges, very restrained shallow dimensional shading. Crisp silhouette with broad simple surfaces, not a glossy inflated toy. A strong one-color silhouette should remain possible.
Scene/backdrop: Plain uniform near-white #F7F9FC background, opaque.
Composition/framing: Square composition, one centered icon occupying roughly 65 percent of the canvas width and height, ample even clear space. Straight-on front view, no perspective scene. No text or icon sheet.
Color palette: Clear sky blue #199FE6, refined deeper azure #0878B2, small restrained highlights approaching #67CFFF. No green, violet, rainbow, or black.
Constraints: One polished symbol only. No wordmark, letters, numbers, annotations, frame, rounded-square app tile, watermark, mockup device, drop-shadow pedestal, or extra symbols. Do not reuse a folded A or wing motif. No literal people, hands, handshake, robot head, brain, gear, circuit board, sparkles, generic infinity knot, paperclip, or diagonal slash pair. Shape must read when reduced to 24 pixels.
