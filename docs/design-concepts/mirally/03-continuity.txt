Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original desktop assistant app icon concept, single isolated brand symbol
Primary request: Create an original refined app symbol for Mirally, a personal AI partner from MIRA (Machine Intelligence Research and Applications) Lab. Mirally combines MIRA and ally. The single idea is continuity: a calm assistant relationship that carries understanding from one conversation or task to the next.
Subject: One continuous thick blue stroke makes a compact, subtly asymmetric open loop around a generous empty center. Its two rounded terminals nearly meet but remain clearly separate; the ending gently approaches the beginning at an offset, expressing a conversation being picked up again. The outline has a distinctive soft angular turn and carefully varied curvature, not a plain circle. Quiet, self-contained, intelligent, approachable. No central dot, orbiting object, arrowheads, spikes, or secondary marks.
Style/medium: Premium vector-friendly technology identity, precise and minimal. Restrained shallow bevel or satin gradient supports the shape. Broad smooth surfaces and an instantly recognizable silhouette that also works in a single flat color. No bulky inflated tube, no twisting ribbon, no glossy glass sculpture.
Scene/backdrop: Plain uniform near-white #F7F9FC background, opaque.
Composition/framing: One mark centered on a square canvas, occupying about 64 percent of the canvas width and height. Front-on view, ample even whitespace. No text or multiple variants.
Color palette: Fresh sky blue #199FE6 with refined azure #0878B2 and subtle #67CFFF highlights, no other hues. Bright, clean, technological without neon glare.
Constraints: Do not force a letter, folded wing, or A shape. No text, numbers, watermark, app-tile container, environmental scene, external shadow, or illustrative objects. Avoid power-button, refresh-arrow, loading-spinner, generic chain-link, infinity, and existing browser-logo silhouettes. Keep open terminal gap and empty center clear at 24 pixels.
