Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original premium desktop application icon for OpenAssist, a conversational assistant that can also take action on a user's computer.
Primary request: design the OPEN CORE concept. One bold, ownable emblem: an almost circular embracing band, gracefully asymmetric, open at its upper-right side. Its lower-left body has confident weight and its two broad terminals taper gently. A generous seed-shaped oval core sits just inside the opening, close to the center, cradled by the band. The band and core form a compact balanced silhouette, suggesting an open O, supportive presence, intelligence ready to act. Make the geometry memorable and extremely simple, not a generic ring with a random dot.
Scene/backdrop: clean uniform mineral-white background (#F5F6F3). Square image, one icon only, optically centered, emblem occupies about 62 percent of the canvas, generous equal breathing room.
Style/medium: top-tier contemporary software brand design, precision curves, strong flat silhouette with very restrained tactile depth. Rich forest green (#245541) body, a subtly lighter sage-green core. Subtle satin ceramic surface and one soft broad highlight, almost vector-clean edges. Sophisticated, inviting, calm and capable. The shape must still work as a solid monochrome logo at 24 pixels.
Composition: straight-on orthographic view, no perspective. The open negative space must be wide and deliberate. Two primary masses maximum. No tiny details.
Text: none. No letters, captions or wordmark.
Avoid: gear teeth, loading spinner, power button, orbiting planet, robot face, eyes, AI sparkle, flower, OpenAI knot, infinity sign, generic chat bubble, shield, ornate chrome, glass refractions, neon glow, shadows that obscure the silhouette, mockup board, additional icons, watermark. Do not enclose it inside another badge or square plate.
