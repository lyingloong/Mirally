Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original premium desktop application icon for OpenAssist, an assistant that lives beside your work and opens access to useful capabilities.
Primary request: design the OPEN PORTAL concept. A bold soft rectangular arch with a broad rounded top and an intentional open lower-right corner. One short broad base sweeps outward to the right, suggesting the assistant unfolding from the side of a screen. The central empty space is large and beautifully proportioned. The whole symbol is one compact continuous shape, quietly architectural yet warm, a distinctive open O with a forward invitation. Design an ownable silhouette rather than a literal picture of a doorway.
Scene/backdrop: clean uniform mineral-white background (#F5F6F3). Square image, one icon only, optically centered, emblem occupies about 62 percent of the canvas, generous equal breathing room.
Style/medium: top-tier contemporary software brand design, precision curves, strong flat silhouette with very restrained tactile depth. Rich forest green (#245541) main body, a restrained muted sage-green edge at the outward opening. Subtle satin ceramic surface and one soft broad highlight, almost vector-clean edges. Calm, confident, inviting, technically refined. The shape must still work as a solid monochrome logo at 24 pixels.
Composition: straight-on orthographic view, no perspective, one primary mass, generous open cut, thick structural strokes with softly rounded corners. The negative space is the center of the design; put nothing inside it.
Text: none. No letters, captions or wordmark.
Avoid: literal door handle, keyhole, lock, shield, folder tab, computer monitor, tunnel perspective, maze, QR code, gear, robot face, AI sparkle, OpenAI knot, ornate chrome, glass refractions, neon glow, mockup board, additional icons, watermark. Do not enclose it inside another badge or square plate.
